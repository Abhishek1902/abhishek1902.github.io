The thesis

I've spent seven years making multi-step data systems reliable, observable, and grounded in source-of-truth data — the discipline that decides whether a platform can actually be trusted: evaluation, data quality, lineage, and governance.

That discipline is exactly what most GenAI systems lack. A RAG pipeline is a data pipeline wearing a trenchcoat — chunking is partitioning, embeddings are a transformation step, retrieval is a query optimizer, evals are data-quality checks. The LLM is the new part; making orchestration reliable isn't. That's what I bring — to data platforms first, and to the AI built on top of them.
