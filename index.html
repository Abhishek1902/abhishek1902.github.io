The thesis

Most GenAI systems die in production because the people building them never learned the boring discipline data engineers live in. A RAG pipeline is a data pipeline wearing a trenchcoat — chunking is partitioning, embeddings are a transformation step, retrieval is a query optimizer, evals are data-quality checks.

I've spent seven years making multi-step systems reliable, observable, and grounded in source-of-truth data. The LLM is the new part; the discipline of making orchestration reliable isn't. That's what I bring to AI platforms.
